# AIP Style and Guidance

## Guidance

- Prefer short, imperative rules with **must** / **should** / **may**.
- Include concrete HTTP and JSON (or OpenAPI YAML) examples in the AIP itself.
  Do not rely on external repositories for normative examples.
- Avoid protobuf samples unless illustrating an internal contrast; the
  normative surface is HTTP/OpenAPI design guidance.
- When amending an AIP, update related AIPs in the same change set, then bring
  implementers into compliance.
